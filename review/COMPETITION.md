# Visual entry research · October 23, 2026

Source: [official public collection](https://trendspider.com/trading-tools-store/collection/hackathon26/). Public listings only; unpublished entries are inaccessible. The initially visible collection contains 12 entries and is not a complete census. Installation counts are not judging results.

[Hokusai Wave](https://trendspider.com/trading-tools-store/indicators/6ab180-hokusai-wave/) turns trend strength into an artistic wave display. The collection also includes Foresight's projected paths, Four Horsemen's macro gauges and VECTR's composite score. Illustration, projections and dashboards are already represented; their authors' usefulness/performance claims were not independently verified.

**Strata's direction:** observed-price distributions. Its terrain consists of real historical closing-price quantiles. Three profiles share price bins and density scales so the migration and overlap of recent and longer samples can be compared directly. Every main visual has a stated numerical meaning.

This differs from the reviewed wave illustration and projected-path concepts. It does not establish uniqueness against every entrant. Quantiles and histograms are conventional methods. No competitor source code or artwork was copied into this project.

The design targets visual impact through layered contours and restrained typography, and clarity through common scales, named windows, numerical overlap and historical-only wording. It avoids BUY/SELL markers, forecasts and unsupported profitability claims.

Technical source: [official scripting documentation](https://platform-docs.trendspider.com/scripting/docs/), especially Painting Indicators, Chart Overlays and the `paint`, `fill`, `paint_overlay` and `describe_indicator` APIs. Overlay chart configurations contain serializable data only.
