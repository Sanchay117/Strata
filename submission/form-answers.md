## Short description

Strata converts price history into a color-coded atlas. Layered contours map the previous 60 closes, and three aligned profiles compare 20-, 60- and 120-bar distributions using identical price and density scales. Teal, violet and amber distinguish the time horizons. Median migration, middle-half overlap and historical close rank help traders identify concentration, dispersion and changes in price context. Dark and light palettes and a compact layout keep the chart readable. Calculations use completed bars and exclude the current candle. Strata describes observed history; it does not forecast prices or generate buy/sell signals. #TSBuild26
