Contours divide the previous 60 closing prices into ordered percentiles. Broad shelves show historical spread; compressed contours show prices gathering.

02 / THREE MEMORIES

20, 60 and 120 bars share the same price and density scales. Compare the distributions directly. Their middle-half overlap measures shared price territory.

03 / WHAT THIS IS

A description of observed prices. Equal weight per close; no volume, holder positions, forecast, buy/sell signals or return claim. Gaps and bin choices affect the picture.
